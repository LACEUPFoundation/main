LACE UP Foundation is a 501(c)(3) youth development organization. Our mission is empowering the youth and building unity in the community. We are headquartered in metro Atlanta, with annual programming in New York, Los Angeles, and Berlin, delivered inside partner schools, community centers, and facilities, which keeps overhead low and places young people in professional environments rather than simulated ones. Participation is free. In 2026 we reached more than 600 young people, students, and families.

What we do

Sport is the door. What's behind it is everything else.

Three programs, delivered inside the schools, centers, and facilities our partners already have. We track academic indicators, retention, and professional exposure from intake, and report outcomes annually.

Sports PerformanceAges 5–13 · every sport

Speed, strength, and fundamentals that carry over, whether a young person plays basketball, football, flag football, runs track, or has not yet chosen a sport. We train on courts, fields, and tracks, because that is how athletes actually get built. Certified trainers from Forward Fitness and Sports Performance lead every session.

CreativityExpert-led labs

Young people make something real alongside a working professional. A sneaker design, a short film, a story they draw into a flipbook. They walk out holding it, and they leave having met someone who does that work for a living.

Technology & LeadershipSix-week series

Teams build a technology project from scratch and present it at the LACE UP Showcase. Every participant holds a leadership role the team depends on. Not a title. An actual job.

Our story

Why I started this

In 2016 I took $100 from a college refund check and put it into a tournament weekend. I did not have a plan or a board or a budget. What I had was a conviction that the young people in my community deserved more than what was being handed to them.

That weekend turned $100 into $4,000, because when you invest in young people, the community shows up. For ten years I funded this myself, alongside donations from family and friends. A founder who will not back her own vision cannot ask anybody else to.

Youth development has become expensive, and somewhere along the way a lot of people started treating kids like inventory. That is not what we do here.

Brooke ThomasFounder & Chief Executive Officer

LACE UP 25

Twenty-five young people. Every Saturday. Everything we have.

We do not hold tryouts. Young people come to us because an adult who knows them puts their name forward: a teacher, a counselor, a coach, a parent, a guardian, or a mentor.

We select on commitment and character, not on talent and not on what a family can afford. Twenty-five is the cap on purpose. We keep it small so no one gets lost.
